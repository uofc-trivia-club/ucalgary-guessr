How to play

Study the campus photo, then select a building or place a pin on the map. You can change your selection before pressing Submit guess.

Use the mouse wheel over the photo to zoom in or out. When zoomed in, click and hold to drag the photo in any direction. Each new photo starts fully zoomed out.

A correct building earns 5,000 points. Other guesses score based on distance. Random games have five photos.

Daily resets at midnight Mountain Time. Weekly resets Sunday at midnight Mountain Time. Both timers count only active time on that challenge page. Log in before playing for a ranked attempt. Guests can play, but their results are unranked.
