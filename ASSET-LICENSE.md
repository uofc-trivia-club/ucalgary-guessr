# Asset terms

The ISC source-code license does not cover photographs, backgrounds, fonts,
maps, icons, logos, or other files under `public/assets/`. Their respective
rights holders retain their rights. This repository does not grant a separate
license to reuse those assets.

The game's photographs (`public/assets/photos/`) are not distributed with this
repository and no license is granted for them. Older commits may contain a small
number of early photographs; they are covered by this notice too, and no
license is granted to reuse them.

Rights to other assets, including fonts, maps, icons, logos, and backgrounds,
must be verified separately before public release. Inclusion is not evidence
of permission. Verify both repository distribution and website display, and
replace or remove assets whose intended use is not permitted.

For new assets, record the repository path, source URL or creator, license or
permission reference, and required attribution. Keep private permission
correspondence outside the public repository.

Original photo archives belong outside `public/` and outside Git, for example
in `.local/photo-originals/`, with a separate private backup. Only the optimized
images needed by the game belong in the deployed photo collection.
